# Five-minute Asterfield demonstration

First follow [safe installation and Windows commands](product-refinement.md). Select **Asterfield Retail & Services · Sample company**. All 21 example invoices have already passed through the real worker during installation; the history is immediately useful. Files are in `backend/demo/asterfield-v1`.

1. **Overview (30 seconds).** Show unique invoices versus financial decisions and the exception entry point. Explain that processing completion and financial validation are separate, and that InvoiceGuard reserves capacity but never executes payment.
2. **Happy path (60 seconds).** Open Invoice processing. Choose Conservative extraction and upload `01-office-supplies.pdf` (Willowbridge, WB-261101, AF-PO-2026-001). It opens the existing validated run: INR 2,950 against INR 29,500 authorised; INR 26,550 remains. Explain exact-document replay. Expand source evidence and compare invoice/PO values. Do not claim a fresh execution occurred on replay.
3. **OCR (45 seconds).** Open LF-261106, `06-cleaning-scan.pdf`. Show the recorded OCR stage, timestamp, extracted values and original image PDF. `07-packaging-photo.pdf` is the photographed sample, also with actual OCR. The raw event history is available in Administrator technical details.
4. **Partial billing and overrun (60 seconds).** Cedarline invoices `02-it-support-october.pdf` and `03-it-support-november.pdf` reserve INR 885 and INR 1,327.50 against AF-PO-2026-003's INR 5,310. INR 3,097.50 remains. `04-it-support-over-budget.pdf` requests INR 3,540 and is BLOCKED: INR 442.50 over the remaining capacity. It adds no reservation. Show the PO's actual commitment history.
5. **Missing reference and review (45 seconds).** RC-261111, `11-catering-missing-reference.pdf`, has no PO reference. It requires review and reserves nothing. Open Exception review, source PDF and recorded issues. Do not invent an email-sourced reference: this example is a manual PDF. Saving a correction performs full financial revalidation; do not save an arbitrary PO just to make the demo green.
6. **Duplicates and history (40 seconds).** `20-office-supplies-copy.pdf` has different bytes and the same Willowbridge invoice identity: BLOCKED business duplicate with no additional reservation. Open Activity History and the retained original records. Finish by noting that all companies are fictional.

Optional: `08-printing.pdf` is tax-inclusive and validated; `21-supplier-order-mismatch.pdf` is blocked because Cedarline's invoice references Willowbridge's order. Suspended/inactive suppliers and a closed PO are represented by files 17–19.

To demonstrate a **new live execution**, upload a new invoice with a unique supplier invoice number, supported fields, matching SKU, and an amount within the currently displayed PO capacity. Do not overwrite existing sample PDFs: that would invalidate their manifest hashes. A fresh valid invoice consumes real remaining capacity in this workspace. Use an isolated fresh version/workspace for repeatable recordings rather than resetting commitments.

If Gmail is configured on the Windows computer, demonstrate a newly delivered PDF in Email Intake and watch its actual stages. Otherwise state that the recorded automated tests used a provider mock. Do not describe Gmail or Gemini as live-tested without observing a real provider call in that environment.
